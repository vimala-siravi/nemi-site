Know what's coming

A range you can trust, not a guess dressed up as a date.

Nemi shows when your next period is likely, with the most likely day. Irregular cycles get a wider range, not a confident wrong date. The fertile window is marked as an estimate.

Log a day

A few taps, then back to your day.

Bleeding, pain, mood and body symptoms with clear icons. Turn on more when you want it, and switch off anything you never use.

Understand your cycle

Your patterns, in plain words.

Average cycle and period length, your shortest and longest cycles, and a one-page summary to show your doctor.

Private by design, not by promise.

Questions

Anything else? Write to privacy@nemicycle.com.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, and everything you need to track your cycle is free.

What if I change phones?

Back up to a password-locked file and restore it on the new phone.

Can I use it for birth control?

No. Nemi's fertile window is an estimate, not a method of birth control.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Delete everything at any time in Settings.

Does Nemi use the internet?

No. On Android it doesn't even ask for internet access, so you can check it yourself in your phone's app settings.
